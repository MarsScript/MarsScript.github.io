Latest • v4.2 Friends, Newest Chats & Timed Alerts

No Auto-Following, Auto-Friend Fareed & Apple Messages Order

  • Manual Following Only: Removed automatic follow-everyone so accounts start at 0 Following until you choose to follow someone.

  • Apple Messages-Style DM Sorting: Conversations in DMs & Groups automatically sort with the newest chat and latest message preview at the top.

v4.1 Smart Aspect-Ratio Player

Adaptive Widescreen (16:9) & Vertical (9:16) Clips

  • No More Cropped Videos: Widescreen YouTube-style videos and photos open in a wide 16:9 theater card with an ambient blurred backdrop.
  • Full Vertical Support: Tall 9:16 clips fit their exact portrait proportions automatically.
v4.0 Social & TikTok Profile Update

  • TikTok-Inspired Profiles: Added Following, Followers, Likes, and Friends counters alongside a 400-character max Bio and Posts/Favorites/Liked tabs.
  • Display Names (3-Day Cooldown): Customize your display name while keeping your permanent @username handle locked.
  • Admin Moderation & 10 Themes: Admins can delete clips, ban accounts, and restart servers cleanly; everyone gets 10 custom themes in Profile Settings.
v3.0 Media & Stability Update

Chat Photo Sharing, Fareed Admin & Zero-Flash Polling

  • Photo Uploads in Chat: Added image attachments with instant previews and full-screen lightbox viewing in Global Chat and DMs.
  • Zero-Flash Sync: Smart message diffing so chat feeds never flicker or flash.
